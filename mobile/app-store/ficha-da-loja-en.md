página em português continua igual pra quem está em português. No App Store

## Descrição — máx. 4.000

```
Spot keeps the places you've been — with what YOU thought of them — and shows you where your friends would send you.

Not reviews from strangers. Your list, and your friends' lists.

SAVE A SPOT IN SECONDS
Type the name of a restaurant, hotel or experience and Spot brings the address, photos, phone and hours. Mark it as "Been" or add it to your "Wishlist", give it up to five stars and write the one tip that matters. The trip and the city are organized for you.

YOUR FRIENDS, NOT THE WHOLE INTERNET
Friendship is by accepted request or invite link. There are no public profiles. In your feed you see the spots your friends have been to, with their photo, rating and note — and you save them to your list in one tap. Comment on a friend's spot to ask what they thought.

PLAN WITH FRIENDS
Pick the countries and cities you're going to and Spot gathers everything your friends saved there, sorted by who went and how they rated it. Build your trip from it, or send the list to the group chat.

EXPLORE
Search a street, a neighborhood, a city or a country and see what's highly rated there, next to what your friends saved nearby.

PASTE YOUR NOTES
Have places scattered in your phone notes, WhatsApp or Instagram? Paste the text and Spot finds each place. You review before saving.

YOUR MAP
Every country you've been to fills in on your world map, with your count of countries, cities and spots. Mark countries and cities from before you used the app — no spots needed.

WHAT SPOT DOESN'T DO
No ads. No public feed. Spot doesn't sell your data or show your profile to people you haven't accepted.
```

## O que falta pra ficha em inglês ficar completa

- **Capturas de tela em inglês.** As de hoje mostram o app em português. A receita
  em `ferramentas/capturas-loja/` precisa rodar com `spot_idioma=en` no aparelho
  de captura (o app já fica em inglês com isso).
- **A /sobre, a /privacidade e os termos** seguem em português. A Apple aceita
  política de privacidade em outra língua, mas o certo é traduzir antes de
  divulgar fora do Brasil.
